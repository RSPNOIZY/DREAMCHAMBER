### Tennessee ELVIS Act (2024) — The Strongest US Law

The ELVIS (Ensuring Likeness Voice and Image Security) Act is the strongest US voice protection law and applies directly to AI-generated likeness. Key provisions:

- Creates a federal cause of action for voice impersonation
- Applies to identity appropriation "to such a degree as to be recognizable"
- 10-year protection window post-mortem (extendable)
- Attorney fee recovery under 17 U.S.C. § 1203(b)(5)
- Applies to AI synthesis without explicit consent
- Private right of action with statutory damages
- No requirement to prove commercial harm (differs from state laws)
- Burden of proof on defendant for "fair use" claims

**NOIZY leverage**: Tennessee ELVIS provides federal grounding for voice-as-property. When a Voice DNA session is registered in HEAVEN, it creates dated proof of original identity. Any synthesis detected outside of consented territory triggers ELVIS violation reporting. The law itself validates our entire consent model as the legally required standard.

### 3-Layer Watermarking Architecture

**Layer 1: Spectral watermarking** (inaudible to human ear, machine-readable)
- Frequency-domain embedding (DWPT: Discrete Wavelet Packet Transform)
- Encodes: consent_token, synthesis_date, territory_code, version
- Survives compression (MP3, AAC, Opus at standard bitrates)
- Detectable via Fourier analysis or specialized tools
- Extraction doesn't affect audio quality (< 0.2dB SNR loss)
- Unique per synthesis (creates fingerprint of origin)

**Layer 2: Metadata watermarking** (C2PA manifest embedded in file)
- Full provenance chain (training data → synthesis parameters → consent scope)
- Verifiable via cryptographic signature
- Persists through format conversion
- Human-readable via consumer tools (Adobe suite, Microsoft apps)
- Tamper-evident (signature invalidates if metadata modified)
- Timestamp-locked (creation date immutable)

**Layer 3: Ledger watermarking** (append-only hash chain)
- Every synthesis request logged to `noizy_ledger` with immutable hash
- Indexed by output hash (sha256 of audio file)
- Queryable: "Is this audio in NOIZY ledger?"
- Forensic evidence chain for litigation
- Cross-references to C2PA manifest and spectral watermark

Together, these three layers create a **consent evidence trinity**:
- Can you hear it? (Spectral: no, but specialized tools can read it)
- Can you read it? (C2PA: yes, natively in Adobe/Microsoft/Google)
- Can you verify it? (Ledger: yes, HEAVEN API with cryptographic proof)

**Forensic reconstruction**: Even if one layer is stripped, the other two provide proof:
- Stripped C2PA but spectral watermark intact? Ledger still contains hash.
- Stripped spectral watermark but C2PA intact? Ledger + C2PA signature prove origin.
- Transcoded format but ledger accessible? Biometric match + consent scope verify legitimacy.
