**Why This Format Matters**

**Frequency Response:**
- 48 kHz sample rate captures everything up to 24 kHz (beyond human hearing range)
- Compressed formats (MP3, AAC) lose frequencies above 16–18 kHz
- For a voice estate that lasts 100 years, this matters: future archivists will hear details we cannot

**Bit Depth:**
- 32-bit floating-point captures dynamic range from the loudest to the quietest moment
- 16-bit CD quality loses precision in the quietest 20% of the dynamic range
- The whispers, the 65 Hz heartbeat, the 396 Hz frequency—all recorded with full fidelity
- No loss, no approximation, no "good enough"

**Archival Rationale:**
- WAV is container-agnostic: no codec, no compression, no proprietary dependencies
- In 2126, WAV will still decode. MP3 patents will be irrelevant
- 48 kHz / 32-bit becomes the benchmark for "uncompressed voice"
